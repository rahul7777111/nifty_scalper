## Ranked Drawback List

### 🔴 BLOCKER — Must Fix Before Micro-Live

#### 1. Model Profitability Below Cost Gate (No Deployable Model)
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Category:** A. Model signal quality
- **Evidence:** `retraining_audit_20260607_212417.json` — 0/82 models pass `cost_1.5x_pf >= 1.0`. Best model (hist_gradient_boosting) achieves PF=1.29 on train but was NOT selected for deployment. Best candidate that was selected (logistic_regression) has PF=1.0 exactly with only 36 trades — not statistically meaningful.
- **Evidence:** `unseen_holdout_paper_trade_20260607_076000.json` — All 5 evaluated configs fail on unseen holdout. Best holdout PF = 0.908 (stress-2x → PF 0.684). Sharpe is negative across all configs.
- **Affected Files:** `models/`, `src/strategy.py`, `data/processed/nifty_option_chain_cost_aware_edge_dataset_20260606_211845.csv`
- **Fix Plan:** Retrain with larger train window, improve feature engineering, adjust label definition, or lower cost-gate threshold — but NOT lowering thresholds per constraints.

---

### 🟡 HIGH SEVERITY — Significant Risk

#### 2. Paper Readiness BLOCKED
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Category:** B. Paper forward-test evidence
- **Evidence:** `paper_readiness_report_20260607_212137.json` — decision: `BLOCKED`. Full run completed but no model passed paper readiness.
- **Affected Files:** `src/strategy.py`, `src/config.py`
- **Fix Plan:** Resolve why no model passes paper readiness before any live/paper trading is considered.

#### 3. max_daily_loss Does Not Include Unrealized MTM
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Category:** F. Risk controls
- **Evidence:** `risk_control_audit_20260607_144741.md` Gap 1 — `_risk_status()` only checks `state.realized_pnl`. An open position with large adverse move is NOT counted against the daily loss limit.
- **Affected Files:** `src/strategy.py` (`_risk_status()` ~line 12222)
- **Fix Plan:** Add `realized_pnl + unrealized_pnl` to daily loss check, called at every loop iteration.

---

### 🟡 MEDIUM SEVERITY

#### 4. Spread/Premium Filters Disabled by Default
- **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Category:** F. Risk controls
- **Evidence:** `risk_control_audit_20260607_144741.md` Gaps 2 & 3 — `entry_max_bid_ask_spread_pct=0` (disabled), `entry_max_bid_ask_spread_abs=0` (disabled), `entry_min_option_premium=0` (disabled).
- **Affected Files:** `src/config.py`
- **Fix Plan:** Set `entry_max_bid_ask_spread_pct=2.0`, `entry_max_bid_ask_spread_abs=5.0`, `entry_min_option_premium=5.0`.

#### 5. opening_max_move_pct Too Tight (0.40%)
- **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Category:** F. Risk controls
- **Evidence:** `risk_control_audit_20260607_144741.md` Gap 4 — 0.40% on NIFTY (~40–50 pts) blocks all entries on trending open days for 45 minutes.
- **Affected Files:** `src/config.py`
- **Fix Plan:** Relax to 0.75–1.0% for NIFTY options scalping.

#### 6. No External Kill-Switch Env Var
- **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Category:** F. Risk controls
- **Evidence:** `risk_control_audit_20260607_144741.md` Gap 5 — no `MSTOCK_KILL_SWITCH` env var to halt trading without code restart.
- **Affected Files:** `src/strategy.py`
- **Fix Plan:** Add `MSTOCK_KILL_SWITCH=1` check at top of `run_forever` and `_decide_entries`.

#### 7. winrate_tracker_enabled Default False
- **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Category:** F. Risk controls
- **Evidence:** `risk_control_audit_20260607_144741.md` Gap 6 — `_strategy_winrate_check()` streak protection is available but disabled.
- **Affected Files:** `src/config.py`, `src/strategy.py`
- **Fix Plan:** Enable with conservative defaults (3 consecutive losses → 5min cooldown).

---

### 🟢 LOW SEVERITY

#### 8. max_daily_loss Default ₹5000 May Be Too Tight
- **Severity:** LOW
- **Category:** F. Risk controls
- **Evidence:** `risk_control_audit_20260607_144741.md` — 1 lot at ₹65 × ₹100 premium = ₹6500 max; ₹5000 limit may trigger before a meaningful trade. Note: This is also a micro-live parameter (Rs500).
- **Affected Files:** `src/config.py`
- **Fix Plan:** Review and potentially set `max_daily_loss=7500` for main mode, keep ₹500 for micro-live.
